# Cover letter tailoring guide

## Rules
1. **Voice:** the user's — authentic, understated, precise, non-corporate. No
   recruiter-speak, no "I am thrilled to apply", no grandiose AI filler. Cut
   anything no human writer would say.
2. **Specificity:** name the role, req ID, team/product, and 2–3 concrete
   proof points from the resume that map to the posting's asks. Generic
   letters fail.
3. **Length:** one page. Short paragraphs.
4. **Never:** mention internal level numbers; invent facts not in the resume
   or profile; claim employment dates that are UNKNOWN.
5. **File naming:** `Cover_Letter_<Company>_<RoleSlug>.md` and `.pdf`.

## Structure
- Line 1: role title + req ID + company.
- Para 1: what the team builds, in the posting's own terms, and why it's the
  right problem for the user (one sentence each).
- Para 2–3: proof points from the resume, mapped to the JD.
- Close: location fit and one plain sentence of interest. No groveling.
